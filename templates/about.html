About Instant CTF

A student-built CTF platform combining the best of CTFTime and CTFd.

The Project

This is a semester project built by a team of 4 CS engineering students at UM6P College of Computing, Morocco. The platform is designed to be the single destination for everything CTF — event discovery, team management, challenge solving, and live scoreboards.
